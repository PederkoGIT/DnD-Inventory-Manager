# DnD Inventory Manager

A fast and intuitive Android application built with .NET MAUI for Dungeons & Dragons players. It helps you easily track your character's gear, manage carrying capacity, and quickly share loot with party members at the table.

## Key Features

* **Smart Inventory Tracking**
  Automatically calculates your total load and visually displays your carrying capacity limit based on your character's strength and size.

* **NFC Loot Sharing**
  Transfer items to other players by simply giving them the item on an NFC tag. Uses advanced data compression to perfectly fit hardware limits.

* **QR Code Scanner**
  Quickly scan generated QR codes at the gaming table to add new loot directly into your backpack without manual typing.

* **Immersive Dice Roller**
  A built-in roller for all standard dice (d4 to d100) with modifier support. Features custom levitation animations and synchronized haptic feedback for a satisfying, physical feel.
